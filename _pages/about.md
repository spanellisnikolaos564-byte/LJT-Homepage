I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I am pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024 - present). I am supervised by Professor Junxian He, who also previously advised me during my undergraduate studies.

I completed my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), graduating in June 2024 with a B.Eng. degree (2020 - 2024).

My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
